Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no server. Open it, enter a ZIP code or place name, and everything else unlocks from there.

Does it work without a signal?

Yes — that is the point of it. The crop database, planner, seed bank, calendar and Plant Doctor all run on the device. Only weather, the hardiness zone lookup and web search need a connection, and the app is built to carry on without them.

Where is my garden stored? Can I move it to a new phone?

On the device, encrypted. Nothing is in a cloud, which also means nothing syncs by itself. To move to a new phone, open Settings → Save a backup, keep the .json file somewhere safe, then use Restore from a backup on the new phone.

I forgot my passphrase.

There is no way to recover it. The passphrase never leaves your device and is never stored, so there is nothing anyone can unlock on your behalf. This is deliberate, and it is why the app asks you to save a backup before you set one.

The assistant says it needs a key. Why?

The "Ask" tab runs on Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude, using an API key you supply. Google's has a free tier that ordinary garden use stays well inside. Get one at aistudio.google.com/apikey and paste it into Settings → Gardening assistant. Note that a Gemini app subscription is a different product and does not include API access. Every other feature works with no key at all.

The camera opens facing me.

It should not — the app asks for the rear lens by name. If it still happens, close and reopen the Plant Doctor, and if it persists please email the phone model; that detail is what makes it fixable.

Days to maturity looks wrong for my variety.

Published figures swing thirty days or more between varieties of the same crop, so the app shows a range and tells you where the number came from. Record a first harvest and it starts learning; after three, it plans with your own average instead. The seed packet always beats the app.

The Plant Doctor diagnosed something confidently. Should I trust it?

Treat it as a well-read second opinion, not a lab result. Image analysis measures colour and pattern — it cannot identify a species or a pathogen, and the symptom answers do the real work. For anything that spreads, a county extension office will confirm it free or nearly free, and the app links to the extension page behind every diagnosis so you can read the source yourself.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Settings exports a real .sqlite file that opens in DB Browser, Python, R or Excel, a plain JSON backup, and a CSV of the journal. There is no lock-in.

How do I delete everything?

Settings has an erase option that permanently removes every bed, seed, harvest, photo and note from the device. Uninstalling the app does the same. There is no copy anywhere else.

Privacy

Nothing is collected, transmitted or sold. The full policy is on the privacy page.

Accuracy

Every figure for germination temperature, seed viability, watering and pH is reconciled against a primary extension-service reference, and the app links to it. The Sources & accuracy screen inside the app lists them all and says how much weight each number deserves. Where the evidence is genuinely mixed — milk sprays on powdery mildew, marigolds against nematodes, Japanese beetle traps — the app says so rather than picking a side.
